# Atlas Solo — backup responsibility statement (DRAFT)

> **DRAFT for legal review.** Proposed to be shown in plain words during onboarding and in the EULA. It matches what the software does today. If automatic backup is added (residual issue R2), update it.

**Your books live on your device, not with us.**

1. Neuradix Atlas Solo stores your business records only on the device where you use it. Neuradix does not keep a copy.
2. If the device is lost, stolen, broken or reset, or Atlas is uninstalled, the records on it are lost. **Only a backup that you made, and stored somewhere other than that device, can restore them.**
3. Atlas does not back up automatically in this version. You are responsible for:
   - making backups with **Back up now** (we recommend at least weekly, after month-end and before every update);
   - saving a copy off the device;
   - keeping your backup passphrase safe.
4. Backups are encrypted with your passphrase. **Neuradix cannot open a backup or recover a forgotten passphrase.**
5. Restoring a backup replaces everything on the device with the backup's contents. Work done after the backup must be entered again. Document numbering returns to where it was at the backup, so you must set it to continue after your last issued invoice.
6. You are responsible for securing the device with a screen lock or disk encryption, because Atlas relies on the device's encryption to protect the live books.
7. Neuradix will help you restore under the support policy, but cannot recreate data for which no backup exists.
