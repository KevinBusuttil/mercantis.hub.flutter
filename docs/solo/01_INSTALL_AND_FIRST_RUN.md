# 1. Installing Atlas and the first run

**DRAFT.** No public download exists yet. During the pilot, Atlas is installed by Neuradix support from a signed build (Android through Google Play internal testing). This guide describes that build.

## Before you install

| Check | Why |
|---|---|
| A supported device. **Pilot: an Android phone with Android 7.0 or later.** iPhone, iPad, Mac and Windows builds exist but are not yet supported (see the platform list in [PLATFORM_MATRIX.md](../release/PLATFORM_MATRIX.md)). | Only supported platforms get help from support. |
| **A screen lock (PIN, password or fingerprint) on the device.** On a computer, turn on FileVault (Mac) or BitLocker (Windows). | Atlas keeps your books on the device. The device's own encryption, which a screen lock turns on, is what protects them if the device is lost. Atlas does not add its own encryption to the live books. |
| At least 1 GB of free storage. | Books, receipt photos and backups grow over time. |
| A place off the device for backups: a cloud drive, USB stick or your accountant. | Uninstalling Atlas or losing the device deletes the books on it. See [guide 6](06_BACKUP_AND_RECOVERY.md). |

Atlas does not need an account or an internet connection to keep your books. It only uses the internet if you later connect Atlas Team, receipt AI capture or an online-store connection.

## Install

1. Open the invitation link that support sent you, and accept the test invitation in Google Play.
2. Install **Neuradix Atlas** from Google Play.
3. Check the name and publisher before opening it. Only install Atlas from the link support gave you.

## First run

When Atlas opens for the first time you see **Set up Neuradix Atlas**.

- **Already have Atlas books** (a new phone, or a reinstall)? Do not create a company. Choose **Restore from a backup instead** and follow [guide 6](06_BACKUP_AND_RECOVERY.md#restore-onto-a-new-or-reinstalled-device).
- **Joining a colleague's Atlas Team company?** That is not part of Atlas Solo.
- **Starting fresh:**
  1. Enter the **Business name**.
  2. Choose the **Country / Tax region** and **Currency**. These set your VAT codes and money format, and cannot easily be changed later.
  3. Choose a **Business type** (for example Services, Trade or Retail). It decides which places appear on your phone bar. You can change it later in **Settings › Business type & features**.
  4. Optionally tick extra areas under **Also add (optional)**.
  5. Choose **Get started**.

Atlas then creates your chart of accounts, this year's financial year, VAT codes and a company profile. This takes a few seconds.
