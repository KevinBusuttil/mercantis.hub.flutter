# 5. Reports and month-end

**DRAFT.**

## Reports

Open **Reports › Financial reports** (or **Money › Financial reports**). On a phone each report opens as a short summary first, with the detail below.

| Report | Answers |
|---|---|
| **Profit & loss** | What you earned, spent and kept |
| **Balance sheet** | What the business owns and owes |
| **Cash flow** | Where the money came from and went |
| **Who owes you** | Unpaid customer invoices by how late they are |
| **What you owe** | Unpaid supplier bills by age |
| **Gross margin** | Profit per item after its cost (stock businesses) |
| **Stock value** | Stock on hand at cost (stock businesses) |
| **Trial balance**, **Stock ↔ GL reconciliation** | For your accountant. Shown in the Accountant view. |

## Month-end checklist

Do these in order on the first working day of the new month:

1. **Enter everything for the month**: invoices, bills, expenses, payments and credit notes.
2. **Reconcile the bank** (**Money › Reconcile bank**) until the Atlas balance equals the statement's closing balance.
3. **Chase debts:** open **Who owes you**, and send statements to customers who are late.
4. **Stock businesses:** count stock if you do monthly counts. Then check **Stock ↔ GL reconciliation** (Accountant view) shows no difference.
5. **Check for outstanding postings:** **Backup & restore** should not show documents still being completed.
6. **VAT:** if a VAT return is due, prepare it in **VAT returns** and check it with your accountant before filing. Atlas prepares the figures. Filing is your responsibility.
7. **Read the Profit & loss and Balance sheet** for the month. Anything surprising is usually a missing document or a wrong date.
8. **Send your accountant the pack:** **Money › Accountant export**, tick what they asked for, **Build export pack**, then **Save file**.
9. **Back up** and save a copy off the device ([guide 6](06_BACKUP_AND_RECOVERY.md)).

## Year-end

Add next year's **Fiscal year** before the year ends (Setup checklist). Your accountant does the year-end adjustments, usually from the Accountant view or the export pack.
