**Where things are.** Names in **bold** are the words on the screen. On a phone the bar at the bottom has at most five places. For a services business they are **Today · Customers · Invoices · Money · More**. Trade shows **Today · Sales · Stock · Money · More**, and Retail shows **Today · Till · Stock · Money · More**. Everything else, including **Setup** and **Reports**, is under **More**. On a tablet or computer the same places are in the side rail.
