# Team multi-device E2E run — 23 October 2026

- Command: `tool/team_e2e.sh` (backend branch `claude/project-thread-ff896d`
  built in release mode, fresh PostgreSQL 16 database, listening on
  127.0.0.1:18080; hub branch `claude/project-thread-ff896d`).
- Environment: a Linux development container. **This is not a pilot**, and
  it does not replace one. Three hub installs share one machine, each with
  its own SQLite file, document engine, Team posting guard, ledger derivation
  and sync cursor. They talk to the backend only over HTTP.
- Result: `test/team_e2e/team_multi_device_e2e_test.dart`, one scenario,
  **passed**.

Steps covered, in order:

1. Owner device A seeds a company, creates the Team company and publishes
   it. Device B (`admin`) and till T (`pos`) join by invitation and pull the
   whole company. All three have the same chart.
2. B creates an alternative-UOM stock item, a service item, a supplier and
   a customer. All three devices receive them.
3. A and B rename the same customer at once. B, the second writer, gets a
   recorded conflict and keeps its edit until it is resolved. T sees A's
   name. B takes A's version and converges.
4. A tries to submit a Purchase Invoice locally. The guard refuses it and no
   GL is written.
5. A posts a purchase through the server (2 boxes of 12 plus a delivery
   charge). Every device holds 24 units, the service line has no bin, every
   trial balance is zero, and the GL is identical account by account.
6. A and B post sales at the same moment. They get distinct official
   numbers, stock falls to 15 everywhere, and the books are identical.
7. B records a payment with the receiving account left blank. It posts to
   the company's own default (Bank), the sale is settled on every device,
   and the books are identical.
8. A's submit response is lost and A retries the same command. The server
   replays the first response, nothing posts twice, and stock is 14.
9. T sells offline under series `TILL1` (it sees 10 at once), then
   reconnects. Two sales are confirmed, a second sync confirms nothing
   more, and every device converges on 9 with `official_number` set and
   identical books.
10. A GL row saved on B is dropped from its queue and never reaches the
   others.
11. Cross-company: B's device token reading company 2 gets 403, and A's user
    token posting into company 2 gets 403.
12. B's cursor is moved beyond the server's log head, as after a restore.
    B detects it, re-pulls from 0, and its books still match A's.
13. The owner revokes T. T's next sync fails with an HTTP error and its
    local books are untouched.

Defects this run found, and fixed in the same change:

- **Draft ids collided across devices.** Naming-series counters are kept
  per database, so A and B both minted `SINV-.2026.-0001`. B's sale merged
  into A's, and B's submit replayed A's posting, losing B's sale without
  any error. Fix: in Team, series ids carry the device's tag
  (`lib/team/team_naming.dart`).
- **The till's stock never converged.** Its locally derived Bin looked like
  an unshipped local edit, so the server's Bin was parked as a conflict.
  Fix: the sync runner marks provisional ledger rows synced, so the
  server's rows fast-forward over them (`TeamSyncRunner._yieldServerDerivedRows`).

Both fixes were checked by reverting them: the scenario then fails at
step 6 and at step 9 respectively.
